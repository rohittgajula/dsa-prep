# The sheet

138 problems in one order. **Pull-based: take the next one when you sit down.**
Nothing here is scheduled to a date and nothing is ever late - a dead week costs
position, not progress. That is the whole reason the old 454-problem calendar died.

| tier | meaning | count |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | the spine - a bad week still does these | 73 |
| **B** | normal sittings | 45 |
| **C** | only if ahead | 20 |

Sections marked **(gap)** do not exist on Hello Interview and were added because
Amazon and Google ask them. Everything else follows HI's own order and links to its lesson.

Run `python3 scripts/plan.py` rather than reading this top to bottom.
